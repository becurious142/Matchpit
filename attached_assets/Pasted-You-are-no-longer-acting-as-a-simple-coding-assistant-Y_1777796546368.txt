====================================================
PRODUCT OVERVIEW
====================================================

MATCHPIT has TWO core verticals:

1. PRIVATE TURF BOOKING
Users can browse sports venues, see slots, pay online, and book turfs.

2. HOSTED SOCIAL MATCHES
Any player can host a public match at a venue by paying a small host commitment fee.
Other players reserve spots by paying reserve fee.
When minimum players join, all reserved players pay remaining final amount.
System confirms match and venue gets paid.

This is NOT a simple listing site.
This is a sports commerce transaction platform.
